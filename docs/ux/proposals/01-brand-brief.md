# Proposal 01: RepVyn brand brief and voice

Status: **approved and written into DESIGN.md** (10 Oct 2026).

## Promise

For people training at home between other things, RepVyn decides what to train today from what they have already done, so they never track recovery themselves.

## Three traits, and what each one changes

| Trait | Means in the product | Say | Don't say |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Decisive** | The app states a conclusion and offers one action. Choices exist but sit one tap away. | "Legs today. They're recovered." | "You might want to try a leg day?" |
| **Honest** | Every number is real. Celebrate only what happened. Say why in one sentence. | "First workout. RepVyn learns your level from every set." | "You're crushing it! 🔥" |
| **Steady** | Rest is part of the plan, never a failure. No guilt, no loss framing. | "Rest day. Chest is still recovering." | "Don't break your streak!" |

## What "premium" means for RepVyn

Expertise without admin: the feeling of a coach who has already done the thinking. Calm, confident and fast. Not luxury materials, not hype.

## Voice rules

- Second person, present tense, short sentences. No exclamation marks.
- Labels are a number plus a noun ("6 sets", "Ready tomorrow"). Buttons name their action ("Start workout").
- Plain international English: no slang or idioms, because readers in India and the US should both read it the same way.
- Body language stays neutral: no talk of "burning", "punishing" or appearance.
- Muscle states are always "ready / recovering / resting".

## Visual identity: kept as is

Greys, one red that means effort, Geist, the body figure as the hero image. Changes are about where the identity shows up (first run, finish, first week), not a new look.
