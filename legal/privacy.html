What we collect

  • Training data you enter: sets, reps, reps left, weights, check-ins, sore muscles, liked or disliked exercises, and your settings (goal, days per week, equipment, body weight, reminder time). This can reveal information about your health and fitness.
  • Account data, if you sign in: your email address.
  • Payment data, if you buy Pro: handled by Razorpay. We receive the subscription status and dates, never your card or UPI details.
  • Usage and error data: app events such as "workout finished" and crash reports, tied to a random ID, through PostHog. We do not send your email, body weight or workout details with them. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, we send none.
  • Notification subscription, if you turn reminders on: a push address issued by your browser, and your time zone.

How we use it

To plan your workouts (this happens on your device), to back up and sync your training across your devices, to run your subscription, to send the reminders you asked for, and to find and fix problems. We do not sell or share your personal data for advertising, and we show no ads.
