Cancelling

Cancel any time in the app: Profile → Account → RepVyn Pro → Cancel subscription. A free trial ends straight away and you are not charged. A paid subscription stays active until the end of the current period and does not renew. You can also email [SUPPORT EMAIL] and we will cancel it for you.

Refunds

If you were charged and did not mean to be, email [SUPPORT EMAIL] within [7] days of the charge and we will refund it in full. Otherwise payments are not refundable for partly used periods, except where the law requires. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay within [5–7] working days.

Delivery

RepVyn Pro is a digital service, available in the app as soon as payment is confirmed. Nothing is shipped.
